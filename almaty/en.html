FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you open at night?

Yes. The Romeo shop at 115 Abylai Khan Ave is open around the clock, every day.

Can it be a surprise, without calling the recipient?

Yes. We only need the full address: entrance, floor, apartment.

Will I see the bouquet before delivery?

Yes. The manager sends a photo of the finished bouquet on WhatsApp before the courier leaves.

How do I pay?

Any convenient way: Kaspi, Halyk and others. You can also pay from abroad: we accept international transfers and cryptocurrency. The manager sends payment details on WhatsApp or Telegram once the bouquet is agreed.

Feedback or complaint

If something went wrong with your order or delivery, write directly to the head of customer care. We will look into it and reply.
